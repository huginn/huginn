## Listing policy

### Hosting providers

A hosting provider is listed on this page while all of the following hold:

- The provider sponsors the maintainer through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/knu) at US$25/month or more on a recurring basis.  One-time sponsorships do not qualify.
- The service runs an official Huginn release or the official Docker image, and is updated within a reasonable time after new releases.
- Users own their data, as Huginn promises: they can export it at any time, for example by running the standard Huginn export tasks or by accessing the database, and the provider does not use it for any other purpose.
- The service page states that it runs Huginn, links to this repository, and keeps the MIT license notice.
- The provider has an identifiable legal entity and a contact address.

Each entry consists of a logo linked to the Huginn page on the provider's site, the service name, an optional short description of up to about 15 words, and optionally a plain text link to the provider's one-click deploy page for Huginn.  No deploy buttons, badges, or other images besides the logo, and no promotional wording.

### Company sponsors

A company sponsoring at US$100/month or more on a recurring basis is listed on this page and in the Sponsors section of the README with a logo linked to its site.  The sponsorship supports the maintainer rather than Huginn alone; a company is listed here when it asks to be.

### Review

All logos and entries on this page are reviewed before they are added.  The maintainer may decline or remove a listing at their discretion.  In particular, listings are declined for businesses in areas such as adult content, gambling, get-rich-quick schemes and similar information products, financial products including cryptocurrency and trading services, and anything that could harm the reputation of the project.

Sponsorship payments are processed by GitHub and are not refunded, even when a listing is declined.  If you are unsure whether your business qualifies, open an issue to ask before sponsoring.

Logo requirements:

- PNG or SVG, with a transparent or white background.
- Up to 120px wide for hosting providers; up to 240px wide for company sponsors.  Height is scaled proportionally, up to 60px and 120px respectively.
- The image is committed to this repository under `doc/imgs/sponsors/`, not hotlinked, so that it cannot change after review.

A listing is removed when the sponsorship lapses or the sponsor no longer meets the conditions above.

To be listed, start a sponsorship and then open a pull request adding your logo and entry to this file, mentioning the sponsoring GitHub account.
